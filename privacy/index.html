Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-02 · Applies to ScanX on the web (v1.0.1) and the ScanX Android app.

Who we are. ScanX is operated by Deepak Dhanusu, Chennai, India. Contact: Deepak.Dhanusu@protonmail.com.

Summary

  • Scanning, the safety check and scan history work without an account and stay on your device.
  • An optional free account (sign-in with Google, Apple, X or an email link) is used for QR codes, Digital ID cards, plans and cloud sync.
  • We collect the minimum needed to run each feature, we never sell personal data, and you can download or delete your data from Settings at any time.
  • The free version is funded by ads from Google. Paid plans are billed by Google Play in the Android app, and by Razorpay (India) or Stripe (international) on the website; ScanX never sees your card number or UPI PIN.

Digital ID: age and fair use

Digital ID cards are for people aged 18 and over who share their own genuine social and professional details. Before creating your first card you confirm this and agree to the fair use policy in the Terms of Use. ScanX cards are for social and digital profiles. They are not a government service: for official or government-related links, documents or identity, use the official government website.

Abuse, scams and bans

Any use of ScanX for malicious activity, threats, spam, scams, phishing, fraud or impersonation is prohibited. We block links that show strong scam signs, suspend cards and links that receive repeated reports while they are reviewed, and ban accounts that break these rules. A banned account cannot create or share anything, its links and cards stop working, and a minimal record is kept so the ban cannot be undone by deleting the account. We may share information with law enforcement where the law requires it.

Payments

Premium and Pro renew automatically each month or year until you cancel.

  • In the Android app, payments are processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. To link a purchase to your ScanX account, the app gives Google Play a one-way code derived from your account (not your email or name). Our server then reads the purchase from Google (plan, status, renewal date and Google's purchase reference) and grants the plan only if that code matches your account. Cancel or change payment method in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • On the website, payments are processed by Razorpay (India: UPI AutoPay, RuPay, Visa and Mastercard cards, e-mandates) or Stripe (international cards), under their own privacy policies and the RBI and PCI DSS rules that apply to them.

All three act as independent payment providers. ScanX receives only the subscription status, plan and dates, plus the provider's reference IDs. Your plan changes only after the provider confirms a payment to our server (a signed message, or a lookup our server makes directly with Google Play).

When a paid plan ends

If a paid plan ends (cancelled, payment failed, or an invite reward ran out), paid features stop straight away and your paid QR links and Digital ID cards show a "paused" page to visitors. For 30 days everything is kept, and renewing restores it exactly as it was. After 30 days, paid-plan content (Digital ID cards, paid QR links, shared contacts and Network listings) is deleted. Codes you made on the Free plan are not affected.

Ads and consent

When you first open ScanX you agree to these terms, including ads that keep ScanX free. Where the law requires it (for example the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland), Google shows a consent message that lets you choose whether ads are personalised. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings. Premium and Pro remove ads. Shared cards and QR link pages never show ads.

Sharing

We share data only as described above: with Google for ads, with Google Play, Razorpay or Stripe for payments, with our hosting provider to run the service, with Open Food Facts or Open Library for product and book codes, with the card owner when you choose to share your details from their card, with Pro members when you list your card in the ScanX Network, and with authorities where the law requires. We do not sell or rent personal data.

Security

  • All network requests use HTTPS. Sign-in uses short-lived signed tokens; every request that changes your data is checked against your account on the server.
  • Your codes and cards can be read publicly only through links you share; they can be changed only by you.
  • Plan limits, consent and abuse rules are enforced on the server, not only in the app.
  • Wi-Fi passwords, UPI IDs and other static QR contents never leave your device.
  • Payment messages from Razorpay and Stripe are accepted only with a valid cryptographic signature, and each one is processed once. Google Play purchases are checked directly with Google and must belong to your account.
  • Link passwords are stored as salted hashes; repeated wrong guesses are blocked.
  • Contact forms, reports, invites and sign-ups for the waitlist are rate limited, and contact forms strip web links to stop spam.
  • Before going live, every feature is tested against common attacks; an independent security test is required before each major release.

Retention and deletion

  • Download my data (Settings → Account) gives you a copy of everything linked to your account.
  • Delete my account removes your account, QR codes, cards and synced history from our servers immediately. Printed codes and shared cards stop working.
  • Delete all my data clears the device and signs you out.
  • Free website links stop working after 7 days. Paid-plan content is paused when a plan ends and deleted 30 days later unless you renew. Daily counts older than 6 months are deleted automatically.
  • Rate-limit counters last at most a day. Audit logs are kept for 12 months. Subscription records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after account deletion, in the payment provider's systems.
  • Account deletion is blocked while a subscription is still renewing, so nobody is charged for a deleted account: cancel first, then delete.
  • Advertising data held by Google follows Google's retention policies.

Children

ScanX is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13. Digital ID requires users to be 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or India's DPDP Act 2023), you may have rights to access, correct, delete and port your data and to withdraw consent. Most of this is in the app. For anything else, email Deepak.Dhanusu@protonmail.com; we answer within 30 days.
