What ScanX does

🛡️

A safety check on every scan

Before you open a link, ScanX checks it for scam signs such as look-alike domains, risky downloads and fake login pages, and tells you plainly: no known threat, caution or high risk.

₹

Safer UPI payments

Payment codes show who you are paying and how much before your UPI app opens, so a swapped sticker on a shop counter is easier to spot.

▦

Make QR codes

Website, Wi-Fi, UPI, WhatsApp, contact, event and more. Wi-Fi passwords and UPI details stay on your phone. On paid plans, website codes can be changed after printing.

🪪

Digital ID card

A visiting card people open by scanning your code or tapping your phone. One tap saves you to their contacts. For people aged 18 and over, with your own genuine details.

🔒

Private by design

Scanning, the safety check and history work without an account and stay on your device. We never sell personal data, and you can download or delete yours at any time.

🌐

Made for everyone

Works in more than 20 languages, in light and dark mode, and keeps scanning even when you are offline.

How the safety check works

  1. Scan. Point your camera at any QR code or barcode, upload a photo, or paste a link.
  2. Check. ScanX reads the code on your phone and looks for scam signs before anything opens.
  3. Decide. You see what the code really contains and a clear verdict, then choose whether to open, pay or share.

Safety checks are guidance from rules and public data. They can be wrong; "no known threat" does not guarantee a link is safe.
